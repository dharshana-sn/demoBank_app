# DemoBank Sections Guide

This document explains the various sections available in the DemoBank application's left navigation panel, how to use them, and their primary use cases.

## Overview
**How to use:** This is the default landing page. It provides a comprehensive snapshot of your financial health, including Total Net Worth, Income, Expenses, and Active Accounts.
**Use case:** Quickly checking your balance, recent transactions, or viewing market pulse insights.

## Accounts
**How to use:** Displays detailed information for each of your linked checking, savings, and investment accounts. 
**Use case:** Viewing account numbers, copying them for transfers, or downloading statements.

## Fixed Deposits (FD)
**How to use:** Allows you to view active fixed deposits, interest rates, and maturity dates. You can simulate new FD returns.
**Use case:** Planning long-term savings and locking in high-yield interest rates.

## Credit Cards
**How to use:** View your active credit cards, available credit limit, outstanding balances, and recent card transactions.
**Use case:** Paying your credit card bill or checking if you are nearing your credit limit.

## Transfers
**How to use:** Contains forms for intra-bank (same bank) and inter-bank transfers. You can select the source account, destination account, and amount.
**Use case:** Sending money to friends, family, or paying for services. 

## Analytics
**How to use:** Provides visual charts (pie charts, bar graphs) breaking down your spending across different categories.
**Use case:** Budgeting, identifying areas where you are overspending, and tracking financial goals.

## KYC Verification
**How to use:** A portal to upload your identification documents (Passport, Driver's License, etc.) to verify your account and increase transaction limits.
**Use case:** Unlocking full account features and higher transfer limits by proving your identity.

## Settings
**How to use:** Allows you to change your profile information, password, and application preferences. This section uses advanced Shadow DOM web components.
**Use case:** Updating your phone number, changing UI themes, or modifying security settings.
