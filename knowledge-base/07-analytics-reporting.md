# Analytics & Reporting

Location: **Analytics** tab — "Visualise your spending and income trends."

## What's shown
- **Spending by category** — a breakdown bar chart of transactions grouped by
  category (e.g. Shopping, Dining, Transport, Entertainment, Groceries, Bills,
  Internal Transfer, Transfers, Investments (FD), Credit Card, Other).
- **Monthly breakdown** — income vs. expenses trends by month.
- A **Flutter-powered analytics module** ("Test Bank Analytics") is available as an
  advanced, richer data-visualization view within the Analytics tab.

## Transaction history & exports
On the Overview and Accounts tabs, customers can view their full transaction table,
which supports:
- Multi-column sorting
- Search with highlighting
- Date range filtering
- Pagination (8 transactions per page)
- **Download as PDF** or **Download as Excel**

## Assistant guidance
When a customer asks "where did my money go" or "how much did I spend on X," direct
them to the Analytics tab, and where possible pull real transaction data rather than
estimating.
