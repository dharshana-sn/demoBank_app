# Test Bank — Platform Overview

Test Bank is the digital banking web application customers use to manage accounts,
move money, open fixed deposits, manage credit cards, complete KYC, and view spending
analytics. All monetary values in the app are shown in **US Dollars ($)**.

## Brand facts
- Product name: **Test Bank**
- Tagline: "Secure. Smart. Modern Banking."
- Headline security features advertised on the login screen: 256-bit SSL Encryption,
  Real-time Notifications, Multi-factor Authentication.
- Application version: 2.0.0.

## Main navigation (left sidebar, after login)
Once a customer signs in they land on the **Dashboard**, which has the following
sections, selectable from the sidebar:

1. **Overview** — net worth summary, account cards, Market Pulse widget, transfer
   forms, Pay to User, and recent transaction history.
2. **Accounts** — full list of accounts with drag-to-reorder cards and a detailed
   transaction table.
3. **Fixed Deposits** — FD calculator, interest rate slabs, and active deposits.
4. **Credit Cards** — card details, quick pay, limit management, statements.
5. **Transfers** — three tabs: Internal Transfer, Same Bank Transfer, Pay to Contact.
6. **Analytics** — spending-by-category and month-over-month breakdowns.
7. **KYC Verification** — identity document upload (Aadhar, PAN, Driver's License).
8. **Settings** — profile details and preferences (currency, branch, notifications).

A **global search** bar and a **notification bell** are always available in the top
header. An **AI Assistant chat widget** (bottom-right corner) is available on every
authenticated page except the login screen.
