# Fixed Deposits

Location: **Fixed Deposits** tab. Lets customers calculate and open a new Fixed
Deposit (FD), and view their active deposits.

## Interest rate slabs (per annum)
Rates effective March 2026. Senior citizen customers receive an additional **+0.50%**
on every slab.

| Tenure | General Rate | Senior Citizen Rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 Days – 45 Days | 3.50% | 4.00% |
| 46 Days – 6 Months | 4.50% | 5.00% |
| 6 Months – 1 Year | 5.75% | 6.25% |
| 1 Year – 2 Years | 6.50% | 7.00% |
| 2 Years – 3 Years | 6.80% | 7.30% |
| 3 Years – 5 Years | 7.10% | 7.60% |
| 5 Years – 10 Years | 6.75% | 7.25% |

The best available rate is currently **7.60% p.a.** (General, 3–5 Year tenure) or
higher for senior citizens.

## Opening an FD
1. Choose the **customer type**: General or Senior Citizen.
2. Choose a **Source Account** to fund the deposit (checking/savings only).
3. Set the **Investment Amount** — minimum **$10,000**, maximum **$50,000,000**,
   via slider or direct input.
4. Set the **Duration** — minimum **7 days**, maximum **10 years (3,650 days)**, in
   Years / Months / Days fields or a slider.
5. Choose the **Interest Payout** option:
   - **At Maturity** — interest compounds quarterly and pays out with principal at
     the end of the term.
   - **Quarterly** — simple interest paid out every quarter; principal returned at
     maturity.
   - **Monthly** — simple interest paid out every month; principal returned at
     maturity.
6. Review the calculated **Maturity Amount**, **Total Interest**, **Effective
   Yield**, and **Maturity Date**.
7. Click **Open Fixed Deposit**.

The system checks that the source account has sufficient balance before booking the
FD; the FD amount is debited from the source account as an "Investments (FD)"
transaction.

## Limits
- A customer may hold a **maximum of 8 active Fixed Deposits** at one time. Once
  the limit is reached, the "Open Fixed Deposit" button is disabled and shows
  "Limit Reached (8/8)" until an existing FD matures.
- Calculations shown are indicative and **do not include TDS deductions**.

- **Capital Safety** — principal is secure with institutional-grade protection.
