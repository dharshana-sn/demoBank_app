# Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I check my balance?**
A: Your balance is shown on the Overview and Accounts tabs, or just ask the AI
Assistant "what's my balance" — it will look up your live accounts.

**Q: Why does it say "Insufficient funds" when I try to transfer?**
A: The amount you entered is greater than the current balance of the selected
source account. Check the account's balance and try a smaller amount, or choose a
different source account.

**Q: Why can't I select my Investment account to send a transfer?**
A: Investment accounts and credit cards are not eligible as a source or
destination on the Internal Transfer, Same Bank Transfer, or Pay to Contact forms.

**Q: I reached the Fixed Deposit limit — what do I do?**
A: Test Bank allows a maximum of 8 active Fixed Deposits per customer. Wait for an
existing FD to mature, or close one, before opening a new one.

**Q: My KYC upload failed. Why?**
A: Confirm the file is a .jpg, .jpeg, .png, or .pdf and is under 5 MB. Try
re-selecting the document and uploading again.

**Q: How do I increase my credit card limit?**
A: Go to Credit Cards → Card Settings → Manage Credit Limit, enter your requested
new limit, and submit. Approval in this demo environment is instant.

**Q: My card was lost/stolen. What should I do?**
A: Block it immediately from Credit Cards → Card Settings, or tell the AI
Assistant "block my card." You can unblock it later from the same section.

**Q: How do I change my notification preferences?**
A: Go to Settings → Preferences and choose Email, SMS, and/or Push Notifications.

**Q: How do I switch between light and dark mode?**
A: Use the theme toggle in Settings; your choice is remembered for future visits.

**Q: I forgot my password.**
A: Use the "Forgot password?" link on the login page to start account recovery.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Yes — see the Security Policy document. Traffic is encrypted with 256-bit
SSL/TLS, and KYC documents are stored securely and used only for verification.
