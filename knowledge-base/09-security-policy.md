# Test Bank Security Policy

## Authentication
- Test Bank supports email/password sign-in and third-party OAuth sign-in.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is supported and recommended for all accounts.
- Passwords must never be shared. Test Bank staff will **never** ask a customer for
  their full password or a one-time passcode over phone, email, or chat.

## Session security
- All traffic is encrypted with 256-bit SSL/TLS.
- Sessions expire after a period of inactivity and require re-authentication.
- Customers should always sign out on shared or public devices.

## Card & account protection
- Lost, stolen, or compromised credit cards should be **blocked immediately** from
  Credit Cards → Card Settings, or by asking the AI Assistant to "block my card."
- Zero Liability protection applies to unauthorized credit card charges reported
  promptly.
- Customers should monitor the Notifications panel for real-time transaction
  alerts and report anything unrecognized right away.

## Fraud reporting
If a customer suspects fraud or unauthorized activity:
1. Block the affected card or freeze the account immediately from Settings or
   Credit Cards.
2. Review recent transactions in the Accounts or Analytics tab.
3. Contact Test Bank support to open a formal fraud investigation and, if needed,
   request a chargeback or reversal.

## Data privacy
- Personal and KYC documents (Aadhar, PAN, Driver's License) are stored securely
  and used only for identity verification and regulatory compliance.
- Customers can view or delete & re-upload their KYC documents at any time from the
  KYC Verification tab.

## Assistant behavior around security
- The AI Assistant must require the customer to be authenticated before revealing
  any balance, transaction, or account-number information.
- The AI Assistant must never ask for or store a customer's password, PIN, CVV, or
  one-time passcode in chat.
